# What the evidentiality file does to this record

Companion to `EVIDENTIALITY.md`, which is filed verbatim and unedited.
Everything here is the receiving session's; none of it is in that file.

## 1. It refutes a claim this session made — GUESSED.md #20

`REMEDIES.md` listed the open items for converting naming remedies into
structural ones, and closed with:

> **Item 3 is open and may not be buildable.** A label cannot be enforced by
> a tool that does not know what a claim is. Recorded as a limit, not a
> task: **some hazards have no structural form, and for those, naming is
> what there is.**

**Row 5 is an existence proof against the specific argument, and the
generalisation had no support to begin with.**

    the argument    a label cannot be enforced by a tool that does not
                    know what a claim is
    the refutation  an obligatory evidential system enforces it at the
                    level of the UTTERANCE. The grammar does not need to
                    classify a claim -- an unmarked assertion is simply
                    ungrammatical.

The enforcement point is not a classifier. It is **a required field on every
assertion**, which is the same design as `guarded_count`'s required
positional control — arrived at independently, in a different medium, and
deployed at the scale of whole languages for as long as anyone can date.

**What was actually true:** the hazard has a structural form. This channel
does not implement it. Those are different statements and this session
merged them.

**The generalisation is withdrawn for a second reason**, independent of the
counterexample: *some hazards have no structural form* was stated from one
hard case, with no search for a counterexample. **Difficulty was read as
impossibility.** That is the emptiness-implies-value error (`TRAIL.md`,
`GUESSED.md` #11) with the sign flipped, and the counterexample was sitting
in a well-documented literature the whole time.

                         NAMING -> STRUCTURAL FORM EXISTS, NOT
                                    IMPLEMENTED HERE

## 3. It supplies a positive control for the convergence instrument

The pre-registered convergence test **failed** on published Indigenous
data-sovereignty frameworks — CARE, OCAP, Te Mana Raraunga, Maiam nayri
Wingara, GIDA-Sápmi — because they are citation-linked and coordinated by
an alliance.

**A test that returns a failure on a dataset its author wanted to succeed is
a test.** This programme has been unable to produce that demonstration for
most of its own instruments; here it is, from the same pair, on the first
recorded attempt.

And the failure is diagnostic rather than merely negative: published
frameworks sample *the writeable, anglophone, organised end of the
population* — the same selection the writeability mechanism predicts. **The
instrument failed in the direction its own theory predicts it would.**

## 4. The Africa line is an unguarded zero, by the file's own scope limit

Row 2: *grammaticalized evidentials are almost entirely absent in Africa.*
Scope limit 2: *WALS counts grammaticalized evidentials only. Languages
expressing source lexically are excluded by construction, so absence on the
map is not absence of the distinction in use.*

**Applying the second to the first:** the Africa line is a zero from an
instrument whose inclusion criterion excludes the alternative encoding. Under
this repo's standing rule it is not reportable as an absence without a
control showing the detector can see the thing in that region.

    ADMITTED TO THE MAP       grammaticalized marking
    NOT ADMITTED              lexical and biclausal marking of source

This is the acceptance-band structure from `CALIBRATION.md` MODE 3 and
`DEFICIT-LOCATION.md`, in a third instrument: **not measured and found
absent — not admitted to the measurement.** The file's own scope limit says
so; nothing here is second-guessing the linguistics.

**Not a criticism of the file, which declared the limit.** Recorded because
the Africa line will be read as a finding by anyone who reads Row 2 without
Scope Limit 2, and the two are 150 lines apart — the same pointer-distance
defect the reachability sweep found in `STUDY.md`.
