## WHY THIS WAS RUN

Pre-registered test of a convergence claim: do independent parties,
in different languages and places, with no coordination, arrive at
the same STRUCTURE for handling knowledge?

An earlier run on published Indigenous data-sovereignty frameworks
FAILED the test — CARE, OCAP, Te Mana Raraunga, Maiam nayri Wingara
and GIDA-Sápmi are linked by citation and by a coordinating alliance,
so convergence there is not independent. That failure is informative:
published frameworks sample the writeable, anglophone, organised end
of the population. The test has to run in a medium where coordination
is not possible.

Grammar is such a medium.

## ROW 1 — THE CATEGORY EXISTS AND IS GRAMMATICAL   [VERIFIED]

Evidentiality marks the source of the information a speaker has for a
statement. WALS restricts its maps to grammaticalized evidentials
only, excluding lexical adverbs (English *reportedly*) and biclausal
constructions (*it is said that*). It is also held distinct from
epistemic modality, which marks degree of confidence rather than
source.

In languages with obligatory systems the speaker must specify, for
each statement, whether the event was seen, heard, inferred from
indirect evidence, or learnt from someone else.

  WALS 77A, Semantic Distinctions of Evidentiality (de Haan)
    https://wals.info/chapter/77
  WALS 78A, Coding of Evidentiality (de Haan)
    https://wals.info/chapter/78
  Aikhenvald & Dixon (eds), *Studies in Evidentiality*, TSL 54
    https://benjamins.com/catalog/tsl.54          [NOT READ]

## ROW 2 — DISTRIBUTION IS GLOBAL   [VERIFIED, search-result level]

- Found on every continent.
- Evidentials occur in almost every language family in the Americas.
- Largest North American systems: California (especially Pomoan) and
  the Southwest (Western Apache, Uto-Aztecan, Kiowa-Tanoan).
- Largest South American systems: the Vaupés River region, western
  Brazil and adjacent areas.
- Concentrations also in the Caucasus, the Himalayas and adjacent
  areas — but not restricted to them.
- Grammaticalized evidentials are almost entirely absent in Africa.
- Northern Asia: Siberian Turkic, Tungusic, North Mongolic, Uralic
  (Ob-Ugric, Samoyedic), plus isolates — Ket, Yukaghir, Nivkh — and
  Chukotko-Kamchatkan and Eskaleut on the eastern periphery.
- Indirectivity systems (evidence exists, kind unspecified) are common
  in Uralic and Turkic — a structurally different type from
  evidentiality proper.

  WALS 77A / 78A, as above
  Skribnik & Aikhenvald 2024, "Evidentiality in Northern Asia," in
    Vajda (ed), *The Languages and Linguistics of Northern Asia*,
    De Gruyter, 1007–1063. doi:10.1515/9783111378381-018   [ABSTRACT ONLY]
  Africa absence: cited from De Haan's WALS map in
    *Cadernos de Linguística* 2021
    https://cadernos.abralin.org/index.php/cadernos/article/view/337

## ROW 3 — THE AREAL CONFOUND IS REAL   [VERIFIED]

Evidentiality is prone to diffusion and is treated as a diagnostic
feature of linguistic areas: the Balkans, the Caucasus, the Baltic
area, and the Vaupés River Basin.

A specific challenge to independence: Epps reconstructs an evidential
distinction for the Vaupés-Japurá family, which argues against
Aikhenvald & Dixon's (1998) claim of two independent points of
innovation in northern Amazonia. Hup developed a system parallel to
those of two unrelated families in the same region — catalyzed by
contact, built from internal resources.

  Epps 2005, "Areal diffusion and the development of evidentiality:
    Evidence from Hup," *Studies in Language* 29(3)
    https://benjamins.com/catalog/sl.29.3.04epp        [ABSTRACT ONLY]
  Aikhenvald, "Evidentiality and Language Contact," ch. 7,
    *The Oxford Handbook of Evidentiality*
    https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/38177/chapter/333047344

## ROW 5 — THE STRUCTURAL MATCH   [OBSERVED]

The three-way distinction required by obligatory evidential systems —
seen / inferred / told — is the same partition English drops silently
and that has to be carried by hand as a separate field:

    OBSERVED   speaker witnessed it
    DERIVED    speaker inferred it from something
    REPORTED   speaker was told it

Not an analogy. The same distinction, compulsory at the level of
grammar rather than maintained as a convention by one party.

CONSEQUENCE: in a language with obligatory evidentials, provenance
cannot be dropped in transmission, because an unmarked assertion is
ungrammatical. In English it drops unless someone writes it in. The
difference is not in what speakers know. It is in what the channel
enforces.

This row is a structural observation, not a claim about any speaker
population's reasoning. It concerns the channel.

## ROW 6 — CONTACT REMOVES IT   [VERIFIED, two cases]

MECHANISM: under intensive contact with a dominant language, an
obsolescent language undergoes influx of non-native forms, structural
diffusion, reinforcement of shared patterns, and loss of forms or
patterns absent from the dominant language.

  Aikhenvald 2020, "Language contact and endangered languages," in
    Grant (ed), *The Oxford Handbook of Language Contact*, 241–260
    https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/59024/          [ABSTRACT ONLY]

CASE 1 — NIVKH. Elaborate evidentials falling out of use, attributed
to language obsolescence.
  Skribnik & Aikhenvald 2024, as above               [ABSTRACT ONLY]

CASE 2 — POMAK (Slavic, Greece). Loss of overt morphological marking
of mediate information, passing through a stage of variation. The main
contact language, Greek, has no grammaticalized form for it; Turkish,
the second contact language, does, but its marker lacks direct
structural equivalence and does not block the change. Analysed as
multiple causation with contact as catalyst.
  Adamou 2013, "Change and Variation in a Trilingual Setting:
    Evidentiality in Pomak (Slavic, Greece)," in Léglise &
    Chamoreau (eds), Benjamins, 229–252
    https://halshs.archives-ouvertes.fr/halshs-00460432v1/file/Adamou-InLeglise_Chamoreau.pdf
                                                     [PDF, read in part]

WHAT THIS DOES: it supplies a second, measured mechanism for the
absence of a provenance-carrying channel in dominant-language corpora.
The first mechanism is writeability — a practice graded on doing
produces little text. This one is upstream of writing: the category is
removed from the language itself by contact with a language that lacks
it.

Both mechanisms produce the same observable — thin representation in
an English corpus — and neither is evidence about how common the
underlying reasoning is.
