# A falsifiable prediction about the invisible set — tested

## The prediction

If **magnitude-violating-expectation** is the only reliably working detector
in this programme, and it requires a quantity to exist, then:

    failures producing NO QUANTITY should be SYSTEMATICALLY ABSENT from
    the caught set.

    purely qualitative errors -- a wrong framing, a dropped scope
    condition, a premise swap with no arithmetic attached -- should never
    appear among the SELF-catches.

**Falsifier, stated before testing: one purely qualitative self-catch
refutes it.**

This is what the pattern-from-noticed-instances could not give. The count of
instances is inert — it is sampled on the same variable that made the
instances visible. **The mechanism extracted from them is not inert, because
it makes a claim about the SHAPE of what is missing rather than the SIZE of
it.**

## Result — the prediction SURVIVED, and the split is clean

    SELF-CAUGHT           8   (1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 10, w1, w2, w3 -- 9 counting
                               the partial)
    of those, quantity    8   ALL of them
    of those, no quantity 0   <- the falsifier. Not found.

    OTHER-PARTY-CAUGHT    7   (3, 4, 6, 9, 11, 12, 13)
    of those, no quantity 7   ALL of them

**Not one purely qualitative error was caught by the party that made it.**
Every single one required a second party. And every self-catch, without
exception, had a number in it.

I searched specifically for a counterexample, because confirmation is this
programme's documented failure mode. **There is none on the record.**

## What this licenses, and what it does not

**Licenses:** a statement about the shape of the uncaught set. If the
mechanism holds, then the errors this session has *not* caught are
**disproportionately the qualitative ones** — wrong framings, dropped scope
conditions, premise swaps — and their absence from the ledger is a property
of the detector, not evidence they did not occur.

That is a **directional claim about invisible content**, which is
strictly more than the instance count could support.

**Does not license:** any estimate of how many. The prediction constrains
*kind*, not *magnitude*. `ABSENCE.md`'s rule still governs — the count
tracks what was catchable, and the ratio to what occurred is UNSET.

## Limits

**1. This is a self-classification by the party whose catches are being
classified.** Same weakness class as the instance count itself. Whether a
given catch "involved a quantity" is a judgment I am making about my own
process, after the fact, knowing the prediction.

**2. n is small and one-sided.** 16 rows, one session, one model.

**3. The split may be explained by something other than the mechanism** —
for instance, a second party has a different vantage and catches different
things regardless of whether a quantity exists. That alternative predicts
the *same* table. **This test does not separate them.** Separating them
needs a case where a second party had no quantity available *and still
missed it*, or where this session had a quantity available on a qualitative
error *and still missed it.*

Row 7 is the closest thing to the latter: a quantity was available (n=49 vs
"several hundred") and it was caught — but only as `CONTESTED`, not as the
full failure. **Partial catch, quantity present. Consistent with the
mechanism, not decisive between it and the vantage explanation.**

## Standing consequence

**A qualitative claim in this record has not been checked by anything.** Not
because it was skipped, but because no instrument here reaches it.

That applies to, among others: every `DERIVED` reading, the target/rendering
split itself, the encoding_form taxonomy, the gate-kind boundaries, and the
layer-conflation reading in `IMPEDANCE.md`.

**The remedy is the same one three separate tasks have now bottomed out at:
annotation by a party who is not this one.**
