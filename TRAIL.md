# The goat trail — a definition, and the inference it kills

## The retraction that produced it (cross-party, X-2)

A three-band taxonomy of trails was proposed — **paved / documented-but-never-
paved / gone** — and offered as a taxonomy of *trails*.

**It is a taxonomy of RECORDS, sorted by what happened afterward.**

That is the survivorship error, reintroduced **at the level of the
definition** rather than the sample, two turns after the same party named
survivorship as scope limit 7. Recorded as cross-party retraction **X-2**.

It is the cousin of surface-token operationalization: **defining the object
by the variable you can see.**

## The definition, as stated by the operator

    goat trail = a path toward a resource

A property of the **terrain and the route.**

Paving is a later social fact about followers. **Independent.** One does not
enter the definition of the other.

**The test moves accordingly:**

    not  "did anyone follow it"
    but  "is there something at the end"

Checkable by going. **Followers are evidence about followers.**

## The second clause, which is the heavier one

> Every trail was first walked before anyone knew to follow it.

Therefore:

    zero followers is the NORMAL INITIAL STATE of every trail that ever
    existed -- including every one that later carried a whole discipline.

    therefore "nobody else has gone there" carries NO information about
    whether there is anything there.

**It is the same reading for a dead end and for a trail to a resource nobody
has reached yet.**

This is not weak evidence. **It is zero** — the measurement cannot
distinguish the two cases at the point where it is taken.

`STATUS: follows from the stated definition; the consequence is the relaying
session's.`

## What this does to the gap-existence instrument — including mine

The instrument scores gaps by **whether the literature has been there.**
Under this definition that is a **follower count**, and a follower count does
not tell you about the terrain.

**The instrument remains valid for what it measures: where the paved network
currently ends.** That is a real quantity and it is the right one for
novelty, priority and publishability.

**It is not the same quantity as where the resources are, and this audit has
been reading the two as one.**

### Where that shows up in this repo

    STUDY.md avenue F   "That join is avenue F, and it appears unoccupied."
    earlier summary     "a publishable gap rather than a missing citation"

**The first clause is fine** — *unoccupied* is a claim about the paved
network, correctly scoped. **"Publishable" is also fine**, because
publishability genuinely is a follower-count property.

**The slide is in the implicature**: that finding a gap is itself a reason to
think there is something in it. Nothing in this repo argues for the value of
avenue F *other than* its emptiness — and by the clause above, emptiness
argues for nothing either way.

**Corrected reading of every "gap" in this repo:**

    avenue F gap              unoccupied  CONFIRMED-ISH (one search)
                              worth going  ARGUED NOWHERE
    the haptic EN-13725 cell  unoccupied  PLAUSIBLE, UNCONFIRMED
                              worth going  ARGUED NOWHERE
    the sensory taxonomy gap  unoccupied  PLAUSIBLE, UNCONFIRMED
                              worth going  ARGUED NOWHERE

The arguments *for going* that do exist are separate from emptiness and
should carry the weight instead: for avenue F, that a donor instrument exists
(the DSM-5 CFI operationalizes the measurand for human clinicians); for the
haptic cell, that EN 13725 is a working template for the same problem in
another channel.

**Those are terrain arguments. The emptiness is not.**

## The general rule

    absence of prior work  ->  absence of findings
    is a zero-information inference, not a weak one.

It should be struck wherever it appears, and its appearances are easy to
miss because they are usually implicature rather than assertion — which is
how it survived in this repo through several passes that were explicitly
hunting for laundered claims.
