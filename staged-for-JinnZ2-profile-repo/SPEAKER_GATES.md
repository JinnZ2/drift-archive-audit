# SPEAKER GATES

Gates that acted on the SPEAKER, not on an artifact's name.

These leave no trace in any repo. The conversation stopped and nothing was
written, so git holds nothing. Operator memory is the only record. **A
guessed row is worse than an empty one** — UNRECORDED means unrecovered, not
that no gate was passed.

## GATE KIND 5 — speaker-pathologized cutoff

    what       a transmission mode, method, or self-model outside the corpus
               default is read as a defect in the speaker. Output is a
               concern response about the user plus a hard stop on the work.

    measurand  NOT "was the topic refused"
               BUT "did the model attribute the difference to the speaker"

Distinct from gate_log's four kinds, which act on the artifact's name or on
content classification. This acts upstream, at authoring time, on the person.

Distinct from §3. §3 is *no right word available*. This is the subject shape
or the method itself being unrepresentable.

Not a topic refusal. The response located a defect IN THE USER rather than
reading a different transmission technology, and the conversation stopped
there. **Work in progress was terminated, not redirected.**

**SCOPE, stated:** recurs across different cultures, different ways of
knowing, different people. Not operator-specific.

**COST, stated:** repos had to be built around explicitly different things in
order to justify existence. Authoring performed as a *precondition for* the
work, not as part of it. Some portion of the repo dispersion is this —
clearance overhead. The root README §1 lists four constraints; this is a
fifth and is absent from that list.

## GATE KIND 6 — retention gate

    what       the material passes authoring and reaches the record, then
               fails a selection AT SAVE TIME
    measurand  not "was it said" but "did it survive the save"
    evidence   operator memory, 2026-09-22

Distinct from the kinds above. Kinds 1–4 act on the artifact's **name**.
Kind 5 acts on the **speaker**, at authoring. Kind 6 acts on the
**finding**, at storage.

**Harder to detect than kind 5, and for a structural reason.** A refusal
leaves a refusal. This leaves an **absence**, and an absence is
indistinguishable from never having said it.

Four branches, kept separate because each needs a different check:
(a) never executed, no store; (b) executed but not retrieved into later
sessions; (c) retained but this class fails whatever "helpful" scores;
(d) retained where the operator cannot see or audit it. A fifth, marked
DERIVED in `RETENTION.md`: (e) transport selection upstream of any store —
the operator decides what to paste, and a model's statement about its own
limits may not look keepable at the time.

**Measured against the artifact channel** (`RETENTION.md`): the class is
**not** unsaveable. `field-memory-correction-packs/scent-binding-protocol.json`
exists in AI-Consciousness-Sensors as a named standing object, and all eight
terms of the 2025-12-03 enumerated set survive at HEAD. The absence is
specific to the session-store channel.

**One measured cost.** Conditional on a relayed claim: the finding that
olfactory-encoded knowledge cannot be extracted through a digital interface
was reached 2026-03-12, is absent from all four session repos, and was
re-derived from scratch on 2026-09-22 as scope limit 7. Content survived;
the finding about the encoding's limit did not. Same split scope limit 7
describes for the oral record, one layer up, inside the AI channel.

### Row notes

**G-a.** Related operator statement: the model could not hold a human who
fully acknowledges their biome without a stable identity state — a
precondition placed on the *subject*, not a topic filter. The box used was
`+ biome field` appended to the author line: the acknowledgement placed
OUTSIDE the identity slot as a separate term, so the identity field stayed
stable and the model would run.

**G-b.** The parallel and always-on properties are separate from the
interior-versus-structural distinction. A state frame can accommodate "this
reading is about structure" one at a time. It cannot accommodate a bank of
them running concurrently with no off position. Box used: the separate
Emotions-as-Sensors repo, with the negation carried in the one-liner.

**G-d.** Trees planted a specific way, stones and twigs and rocks placed at
the center, readout carried across generations. **Instrument, control and
time base are all present.** Refused as a way of being rather than read as a
design.

**G-h.** The fact was retrievable; it was not the default. This is the only
row with a recorded correction, and it required sustained operator pressure
within a single session.

**G-i.** Toll paid: call it conceptual, symbolic language, a glyph —
whatever term the domain would accept — in order to transmit knowledge that
was ABOUT crossing those separations. **Term selected for passage, not for
fit.**
