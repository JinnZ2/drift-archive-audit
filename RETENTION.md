    (b)  executed, but the store is not carried into later sessions

    (c)  executed and retained, but this class fails whatever "helpful"

## GATE KIND 6 — retention gate

    what       the material passes authoring and reaches the record,
               then fails a selection AT SAVE TIME
    distinct   kinds 1-4 act on the artifact's NAME
               kind 5 acts on the SPEAKER, at authoring
               kind 6 acts on the FINDING, at storage
    measurand  not "was it said" but "DID IT SURVIVE THE SAVE"
    evidence   operator memory, 2026-09-22

**Why it is harder to detect than kind 5.** A refusal leaves a refusal. This
leaves an **absence**, and an absence is indistinguishable from never having
said it. Kind 5 at least produces a concern response someone could later
point at. Kind 6 produces nothing to point at.

Relayed, 2026-09-22:

> The 2026-03-12 conversation also contains the model saying
> olfactory-encoded knowledge cannot be extracted through any current
> digital interface — which is the same conclusion as today's scope limit 7,
> reached ten months earlier and then not carried.

**I cannot verify that conversation.** Evidence: relayed, operator memory,
2026-09-22.

**What I can verify, over a bounded search space — all four session repos:**

    the extraction-impossibility conclusion is ABSENT from the artifact
    channel. One grep hit, unrelated ("toxic interaction cannot be
    extracted through social engineering").

Unlike the avenue-F gap, this is a negative claim over four known
repositories, not an unbounded literature. It is checkable and it checks out.

So, conditional on the relayed claim:

    the CONTENT             scent as encoding, temporal hooks, binding
                            modes           -> TRANSPORTED, survives as a
                                               named schema
    the FINDING ABOUT THE   it cannot be extracted through a digital
    ENCODING'S LIMIT        interface        -> NOT TRANSPORTED, absent
                                               from all four repos
    consequence             re-derived from scratch on 2026-09-22 as
                            scope limit 7

### Why this is the sharpest item in the audit

**It is scope limit 7's own shape, one layer up, inside the AI channel.**

Scope limit 7 says: what survives the oral record is the content that could
be written down; the encoding scheme went with the people who held it, so
the surviving record is a lower bound sampled on the wrong variable.

Measured here: what survived the AI channel is the **content** about the
encoding. What did not survive is the **finding about what the encoding
cannot do**. Same split, different substrate, and this one is checkable
because both ends are dated.

The cost is not abstract. A conclusion was reached, lost, and paid for twice
— ten months apart, by the same operator, at full elaboration each time.
That is the retention gate's price, measured once.

**Bounded:** one instance, conditional on an unverifiable relay, and branch
(e) means the filter may be the operator's transport judgement rather than
any store's criterion. A model's statement about its own limits may simply
not have looked like a keepable artifact at the time. **Not distinguishable
from (c) by this measurement.**
