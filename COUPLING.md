## A mechanical test — ARITY, not judgment

Retractions 14 and 15 established that this session is unreliable at
category judgments of exactly this kind. So the classification is **not** made
by semantic intuition. It is made by **arity**, which is the same operation
M2 scores:

    ATTRIBUTE FRAME   arity 1. The term takes ONE argument: the unit.
                      "X has a deficit." Complete sentence, no second slot.

    COUPLING FRAME    arity 2+. The term REQUIRES a second argument:
                      what the unit is coupled to / tracking.
                      "X is coupled to ___." Incomplete without it.

**Test: can the term be predicated of a unit in isolation and still be
complete?** If yes, attribute frame.
