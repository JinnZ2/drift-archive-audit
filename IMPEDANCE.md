# The constraint-stack instrument — impedance as a measured variable

`STATUS: instrument specified, one row drafted, today-side NOT looked at.`

## Why this works where "would AI have helped Einstein" does not

The discovery is in the record. **So is the resistance** — referee reports,
rejection letters, funding decisions, conference receptions, symposium
proceedings.

So the design is not predicting an outcome. It **reconstructs a constraint
stack whose effect on a known case is documented**, then asks whether that
structure is present now and whether it has tightened or loosened.

    the unknown moves from the past to the present, where it can be checked

## Five layers, declared as fields — one row per case

    L1  AVAILABLE SCIENCE    what was in the literature, and crucially
                             what was NOT
    L2  CORPUS WEIGHTING     which reading dominated by volume and
                             authority
    L3  INCENTIVES           what a career rewarded; who funded what;
                             publication route
    L4  CATEGORIES / LABELS  which discipline owned it; what it had to be
                             CALLED to be reviewable at all
    L5  DOCUMENTED           the specific recorded friction: rejection,
        IMPEDANCE            delay, dismissal, reframing — who blocked, on
                             what stated grounds

**L5 is the measured variable. L1–L4 are the conditions.**

## The third arm is what keeps it from being a story

Cases matched on stack, varying on **outcome**:

    FAST THROUGH    Einstein 1905
    SLOW THROUGH    Feynman 1948 -> Dyson 1949; drift 1912 -> 1960s;
                    Mendel 1866 -> 1900
    NEVER THROUGH   the unpaved set

**Without the third arm this is survivorship for the third time in this
programme.** With it, L5 becomes a comparison rather than an anecdote: same
impedance, different outcome, what differed.

**The arm is partial by construction.** Cases that left no record are not in
it. Known sign, one-sided — the same shape as the arithmetic-catch bias in
`TESTS.md` T-0b, and stated inside the arm rather than appended to it.

## LEAKINESS — the quantity that actually transfers

The period stacks were enforced **by humans, slowly, unevenly, with gaps.**
Einstein published from a patent office — **that route existing is a
property of the enforcement layer, not of him.**

Today's stack is faster, more uniform, and partly mechanized: metrics, desk
rejection, and now model-mediated filtering at intake.

**So the comparative question is specific and checkable:**

    did the number of INDEPENDENT ROUTES to publication go UP or DOWN,
    and did the VARIANCE BETWEEN REVIEWERS narrow?

**Uniform enforcement is stronger enforcement even at constant
strictness** — a leaky filter passes the anomalous case by accident, and
that accident is how several of these arrived.

## Scope limits — stated BEFORE the today-side is looked at

**1. Order is load-bearing.** The today-reading must be specified from the
historical cases **first**. Reversing it makes the design
confirmation-shaped, **and this programme has already paid for that once**
(`GUESSED.md` #5 — a false-zero table that agreed with the hypothesis under
test).

**2. Enforcement changed on every axis at once** — volume, speed, funding
structure, mechanization. **Attributing a difference to any one axis is not
available from this design.** Same structure as `TRANSPORT×MODEL`.

**3. Pace of knowledge has no agreed measurand.** Do not claim "slower."
Claim a **route count**, a **variance**, a **delay interval** — quantities
with definitions.

**4. Selection on the outcome**, partially corrected by the third arm, never
fully.

## THE OBJECTION WAS CORRECT — which is what makes the case usable

Tidal force and centrifugal force were far too weak to move continents
through a viscous mantle; forces sufficient to do it would tear the Earth
apart.

**That objection was right. Wegener's proposed mechanism was wrong.**

Corroborated by search this session: *Wegener concentrated on developing
evidence that continents HAD drifted, not on what caused them to move*; he
proposed continents plowing through or sliding over oceanic crust, and
**physicists proved both mechanisms impossible** — the crust is too strong
to plow through, frictional resistance too great to slide over.
`VERIFIED to search-result level.`

### The finding is what happened next

    a valid refutation of the MECHANISM was used to reject the
    KINEMATIC claim that the continents had moved.

**Two separable layers, collapsed into one verdict.**

So the case is **not** "they were closed-minded." It is a **layer
conflation** — a checkable structural error with a name, which can be
looked for in live cases.

    the SEPARABILITY is VERIFIED: Wegener's evidence was kinematic,
    the refutation was mechanistic, and the sources state the split
    the CONFLATION reading is DERIVED, and is the relaying session's

## L4 IS IN THE RECORD VERBATIM — usually it has to be inferred

Chamberlin's stated objections: not a general theory of earth behaviour;
does not fit the accepted record of geological time; geophysical evidence
does not support the assigned causes — **and then, separately**, that the
planetesimal hypothesis is an integral part of comprehensive geological
philosophy while Wegener's is not.

**That last clause is not an evidence criterion. It is a
FRAMEWORK-MEMBERSHIP criterion, stated openly, sitting beside the evidence
criteria without being marked as a different kind of thing.**

`UNVERIFIED — wording is from an abstract, not the chapter.`

## THE HISTORIOGRAPHY IS CONTESTED — take the dispute, not a side

Le Grand (*Earth Sciences History*, 1995) argues the standard account is
wrong on two counts: drift was discussed extensively in the US before 1926;
the New York symposium was inconsequential next to the 1928 publication; and
the decisive rejection came from a concerted effort by a few figures he
describes as **deaf to the judgments of foreigners**, finished by Willis and
Schuchert in 1932.

**Two consequences, kept separate:**

**(a)** the impedance may be located in the **publication and a small number
of actors**, not in a conference — **which changes which layer the row
should score.** L5's entry would move from a venue event to an actor set.

**(b)** *"deaf to the judgments of foreigners"* is a **speaker-attribution
variable in a 1995 peer-reviewed history.** If it holds, **gate kind 5's
measurand has a second documented instance, independent of the first** — and
in a field with no connection to this programme.

## The resolution path — and it is the seam shape again

Resolution arrived from **paleomagnetism and marine geophysics** — a
different discipline with different instruments. `VERIFIED to search-result
level`: paleomagnetic work of the 1950s was the first new evidence in favour;
Runcorn changed position in 1956; Hess proposed seafloor spreading in 1962;
magnetic anomalies parallel to oceanic ridges produced near-universal
acceptance.

**Same shape as Dyson paving Feynman.** Same shape as the seam problem
already filed: the resolving instrument came from outside the discipline
that held the verdict.

## What this row gives the instrument

A worked L1–L5 with per-item provenance, an outcome-arm assignment, a
**structural error with a name** (layer conflation) that is checkable in

a contested historiography carried as a dispute.

**What it does not give:** any today-side reading. Per scope limit 1, that
must be specified from the historical rows first, and only one row exists.
